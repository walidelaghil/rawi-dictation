What to look for. Watch the gaps between words. Today the bars drop to nothing between syllables and jump back, which is why the meter looks nervous. The proposal keeps a little of each word as it fades, so a sentence reads as one continuous shape.

It never lies about silence. Stop talking and the proposed meter is empty within about a second. Rise time stays short on purpose: the first thing you want from a microphone meter is proof it heard you start.

The peak marker is the one brighter bar left behind at your loudest recent moment. It answers "am I loud enough?" at a glance, and it can be dropped if it feels like one detail too many.
